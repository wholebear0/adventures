Trip Details

This trail follows along a the PCT and is well maintained and after starting passes over the road after a few minutes, then gradually climbs up out of forest to below the Nipple. From then on there isn't any shade or trees but the views are great in all directions. Leave the PCT trail and go about .3 miles and up about 230 feet to the summit where the views are even better. It was windy so I did not linger but went back down to the saddle then west to climb the other summit. While these peaks have a flat roundish side to them facing the PCT, there is a sheer dropoff and notches on the other side of the ridge, making for some cool scenery. On a Saturday it wasn't too busy and I ran into to only one group going up and several groups going down.
