Teton River, Bates to Big Eddy

08/02/2026 - Teton River, Bates to Big Eddy

The Teton river in Idaho is mostly a flat class 1 river up until its grade steepens into a canyon west of the town of Tetonia by highway 33. This river trip made for a nice rest day through a nice light flow rate. There were no rapids or boulders but a couple shallow spots to avoid in the middle of the river in spots. But the water was clear enough to spot them from enough distance away to avoid them. I parked at the Big Eddy river access then walked back to the Bates river access to put in. There were quite a few folks out, some on rafts and kayaks, and others sitting on paddle boards. There was much wildlife like herons, fish, and even a moose that was standing halfway in the river munching on tall grass along the bank.
