Trip Details

Last night's snow changed plans a bit. Instead of hiking up to Hurd peak I decided to do a more minor summit, Chocolate Peak. In the early morning Hurd Peak was lightly covered by snow but didn't look too bad yet I still headed over to Chocolate Peak, finding some okay views on top. I turned back and finished the loop the quickest way possible.
