Lake Roberts

08/14/2026 - Lake Roberts

Lake Roberts is a small lake in the Gila national forest. The water is greenish in color and not clear but it makes for a nice short paddle. On a late Friday afternoon there were few people, and noise except for thunder in the skies, frogs chirping and birds quacking. A few fisherman had lines in the water and one or two other folks were on inflatable rafts, fishing and moving around the lake.
