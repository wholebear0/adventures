Avalanche Peak

08/07/2026 - Avalanche Peak

Avalanche Peak is a short hike form a roadside trailhead with an elevation gain of about 2,000 ft to its summit at 10,550 ft. It is normally hiked as an out and back but there is an unofficial trail down from the summit to make it into a big circle or loop, which I recommend doing as it offers new scenery. The day was incredible hazy from the wildfires and smoke in canada and the pacific northwest so views were limited unfortunately.
