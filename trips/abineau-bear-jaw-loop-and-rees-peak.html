Trip Details

The first two weeks of October is a nice time to be in Flagstaff with aspen trees turning color. This hike is on the north side of the San Francisco mountains and goes through nice corridors of vibrant yellow aspen trees and pines along the Abineau-Bearjaw loop trail. I made a side trip up to Rees Peak to get a glimpse of the inner basin and saw a lightly snow covered Humphrey's Peak to the west.

Later back at the trailhead and wondering why I saw no one else up at the peak, there a posting on the trailhead info board saying hiking off trail above 11,400 is no longer allowed through at least the end of 2029. The map the forest service shows has the area from Agassiz peak to the south up to Humphreys Peak, then east to Abineau peak as an all red/restricted area. But Rees peak is not red despite the highest point on the summit being a touch over 11,400 at 11,450 ft. Had I known I probably would not have hiked up there but according to the forest services map it is allowed (from the best I can tell).
