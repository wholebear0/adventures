Steeple and KP Rim Trail loop

08/26/2026 - Steeple and KP Rim Trail loop

Given that trails in this area are not well maintained, I tried to make a loop of a trail knowing that in general in the area, trails seem to be less maintained the further one ventures from a trailhead. This hike didnt go far but combined a couple miles of several different trails, but nonetheless I encountered sections of trail entirely disappearing. From a parking lot just off the main road 191, I walked east on forest road 29A for just a bit then headed south on the steeple trail through a burn area. The trail was easy to follow until a cow spring 2.5 miles in where the trail entirely disappeared. Subsequently there was bushwhacking and walking through tall grass and weeds almost up to the KP Rim trail. The KP Rim trail had a nice view into the canyon to the south but it didn't follow along it. Instead there was a nice and dense aspen tree corridor which would be nice to hike along in the fall, but also tons of New Mexican locust shrubs which are covered with annoyingly sharp thorns. After walking west to the KP Rim trailhead I planned to walk along forest road 17c which on a map parallels the highway. I stomped around in tall brush and over many downed trees and was apparently right on top of said road but I couldn't find it so I walked back to the highway and road walked the 1.2 miles back to the car. Only one other car passed so the road walk wasn't too bad, but the hike was peaceful and I saw no one else, but it lacked something interesting which I think would be needed to offset the at times frustrating bushwhack that it was.
