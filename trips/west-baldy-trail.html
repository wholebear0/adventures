West Baldy Trail

08/28/2026 - West Baldy Trail

This is probably the most popular hike in the White Mountains and for good reason. While temps in lower elevation Arizona were above 110 degrees, the temperature here was in the high 50s to mid 60s during the day. There were a small handful of other folks hiking the trail but it never felt crowded. The hike started at the West Baldy trailhead and followed along a meandering creek for a few miles, then up a series of grassy hills, and eventually switchbacked up a steeper section, passing numerous brightly colored red mushrooms along the way. After about 7.5 miles is a junction to the East Baldy trail which one can make a loop of. Baldy Peak itself is on the San Carlos reservation and a permit is needed and can be obtained by calling their office. Up to the junction I thought there would be more views out but there weren't. Probably at Baldy Peak there are some really nice views but I didnt have a permit, which I then regretted, so I headed back down, intent on coming up again in the Fall or next summer with one to fully enjoy the area.
