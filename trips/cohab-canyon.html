Trip Details

Cohab canyon is a very short and popular hike near the capitol reef visitor center. It leads up to two nice overlooks below of the canyon that follows highway 24nthrough the park. Earlier that day there was a small snowstorm leaving a few inches of snow on the ground but had nearly all melted by the time I hiked this in the late afternoon.
