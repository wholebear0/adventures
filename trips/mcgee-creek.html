Trip Details

The weather changed last night as clouds rolled in and temperatures dropped and I awoke to find a heavy layer of clouds surrounding the peaks and what appeared to be some snow on the higher elevations. Intrigued, I headed to McGee creek trailhead on a whim, curious to see how the weather and light would play out during the day. I mentally committed to a short hike, perhaps 6 miles or less since today was my rest day. Arriving at the trailhead I was surprised to see so much fall color, the yellow of aspens illuminated down the valley enclosed by colorful mountain cliffs.

The trail headed southwest then turned left to go straight south, following along the creek. The trail had very gradually slopes which made for easy walking. There were a handful of backpackers hiking back to the trailhead and even a pair of kids about 15 years old who camped the night somewhere over a pass. At one point there was a meadow with the creek meandering against a backdrop of aspens and lightly snow covered peaks in the distance. I continued past it for a quarter of a mile to a footbridge then turned around, admiring the view back down the valley from which I came. By that time the weather turned a bit more dramatic, clouding out the higher parts and blanketing them with snow, and hailing for an hour in the lower elevations where I was walking. It was a very scenic area and I would like to return again in the future for a longer backpacking trip.
