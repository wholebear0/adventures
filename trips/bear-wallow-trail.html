Bear Wallow Trail

08/26/2026 - Bear Wallow Trail

This trail started at about 8,700 ft in elevation so it was cool enough to enjoy in the middle of summer. The road in was unpaved but good and there are several places to camp along the way. The hike descended a few hundred feet past a burn area and pine trees to a creek with not much water. I continued hiking and the trail at times became a bushwhack, requiring the need to push grass and branches out of the way. Eventually the trail itself became wet, muddy, and saturated with water. Combined with the bushwhacking and feeling like I got a good taste of the area I turned around and headed back.
