Trip Details

Cirque Peak has some nice views of the southern Sierra and is not too long of a hike. Start from Cottonwood Lakes trailhead, hike about 1,000 ft on well graded trail with (too many) switchbacks to Cottonwood Pass. Continue a bit, now joining onto the PCT, then pass Chicken Spring Lake.

I jumped off trail at this point, hiking a small boulder strewn ridgeline to an unnamed peak 12,400, then hiking north/northwest just below the top ridge to the saddle just below Cirque Peak, then ascending it. On the way down I cut down the slopes before getting back to the saddle more directly, joining the PCT sooner and walking a mile or so on it back to where I had jumped off on the way up. This way was much easier and faster. I ran into only three other people hiking in the 7 hours or so the hike took.
