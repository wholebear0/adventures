Zapata Lake

08/10/2026 - Zapata Lake

Adjacent to Great Sand Dunes National Park in the Sangre de Cristo mountains are a series of peaks and canyons. This hike ascended South Zapata creek to Zapata Lake. The trailhead is quite busy as there is a campground and falls less than a half mile from it. The trail to the lake is a different story- there was only one other group and it took about 6 hours round trip. The hike starts at about 9,000 ft in elevation and ends at the lake at about 11,800 ft so uphill walking was more tiring than normal. The trail is definitely in need of maintenance. Mid way to the lake there are downed trees more than once per minute which has resulted in numerous side trails to skirt around them, which when combined with the elevation, made for a tiresome hike. It was mostly forest walking with limited views until shortly before the lake at the treeline. The lake itself was a cloudy green color surrounded by grey rocks and peaks, and yellow tall grass. I found it to be lacking something interesting. Some wildflowers or anything with color would have helped.
