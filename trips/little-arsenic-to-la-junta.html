Trip Details

The Rio Grande Del Norte national monument is about one hour northwest of Taos. There is a loop road with some decent $10 BLM campsites along the way. Several of the campsites have trails that go down from the rim about 800 ft to the Rio Grande below. This trail started at Little Arsenic campground then followed along the Rio Grande to La Junta, which is where another river, the Red, joins into the Rio Grande. It was a really pleasant short hike and there were no other people out. There was periodic thunder but I think it was the remoteness of the park and being in the middle of the week helped lessen the crowd. Along the river are several first come first serve camping sites which are really nice. Each one has a shelter with a picnic table and fire pit on level ground. These sites would make for a short and pleasant overnight.
