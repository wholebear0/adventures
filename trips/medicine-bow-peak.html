Medicine Bow Peak

08/08/2026 - Medicine Bow Peak

Chased away from the Yellowstone and Montana area by the crazy amount of smoke that drifted in from wildfires in the Pacific northwest and Canada, I audibled and headed south to the Medicine Bow national forest in southern Wyoming where the air was noticeably better. Topographic maps show an area at high elevation over 10,000 ft with trails and many small lakes and campgrounds. I arrived in the area on a Saturday and the area was packed with people mostly from Colorado. All parking lots and trailheads were over capacity with cars parking 30 deep along the road. This hike was crowded but had great views, walking along a series of high elevation lakes then switch backing up to Medicine Bow peak. Near the top it got very windy with probably 50 mph gusts and some rain drops. Looking up behind the mountains the sky was blue with rain clouds. I hurried up to the peak then consulted a weather radar map which showed incoming rain in under a half hour from the west. The original plan was to hike up and continue west across the flat mountain top to make a loop out of the hike but because of the weather I scrapped the idea and went back down the way I came. Ultimately the rain never really hit but better safe then sorry. It was still a nice hike and there are options to extend this hike by hiking from the east along the ridges or backpacking to/from the other side.
