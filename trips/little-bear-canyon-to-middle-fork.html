Little Bear Canyon to Middle Fork

08/15/2026 - Little Bear Canyon to Middle Fork

There was some rain the previous evening so in the morning a layer of clouds blanketed the lower elevation mountains and valleys. After starting hiking the clouds cleared up to a nice day. The hike began at the TJ Corral trailhead on the Little Bear canyon trail. About halfway through to the middle fork of the Gila the trail and canyon it was in narrowed and was very nice. There is a nice flat area with camping spots near the junction with the middle fork. I intended to do a loop and continue hiking the trail next to/in the middle fork but the water level seemed too high. It was flowing fast and looked deep and I heard that there are at least 30 crossings in the 6 miles or so back, so I turned around and hiked out the same way as in.
