Trip Details

Ever since scrambling up to nearby Castle Rock a year and a half earlier I had been wanting to return to this area and hike up Calico peak. The surrounding area is painted with color, purples and whites, then red and greens, complimented by blue sky. Parking at the Paria movie set trailhead, where at 9:30 am I was the only car, hike west on an established trail through muddy wash and less than a mile later exit the wash into a maze of muddy hills, and continue in a drainage, twisting through a dozen or so bends.

A mile later exit the drainage and hike up a steep rocky slope onto flatter ground to catch a glimpse of Calico peak which has been mostly hidden. Hike towards it gaining some elevation then skirt around to the back (northwest side) of it to locate a weakness to scramble up to the top. There was tons of loose rock that crumbled away when stepped on. The route to the summit was almost as twisty as the drainage, requiring navigating around large boulders. The views at the top were nice and some of the Paria river canyon can be seen.

Sitting at the summit in the shade underneath a juniper and ruminating on the Castle Rock hike a year earlier I found I had enjoyed that one much more than Calico peak. The scrambling there was more fun on solid sandstone while this one was almost like a chore, extending effort to prevent sliding out on loose rocks. But it was still a fun hike and no one else was there.
