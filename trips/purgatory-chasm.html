Purgatory Chasm

08/14/2026 - Purgatory Chasm

Located right off one of the main roads in the Gila National Forest, Purgatory Chasm has an intense name but it is actually a short non-technical loop hike. A few minutes in leads to a sign pointing to the loop in both directions. I headed counter clockwise and walked up a very slightly inclined hill. Looping back around is where the chasm begins. There is some rock no more than 20 feet tall that weaves downhill like a maze. It's short but sweet.. not a slot canyon but a quick and fun stretch of the legs.
